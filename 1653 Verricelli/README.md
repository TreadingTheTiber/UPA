*Note on this entry: Angelo Maria Verricelli, a Theatine moral theologian, treats the UPA question inside his canonization quaestio — Tract. VIII, q. 24, "Whether it is de fide that the Pope cannot err in the Canonization of Saints?" — and the placement is itself the argument: the singular-pope thesis enters (n. 7) as the* ***working model*** *for how a singular gets under a revealed universal. His nn. 4–6 build the machinery: given a revealed universal, all singulars* ***evidently*** *contained under it are de fide credenda (n. 4); the baptized infant is the clean case (n. 5); the consecrated Host is the counter-case — de fide to no one, "not even the consecrating priest," because the conditions (the matter, his own ordination) can be doubted (n. 6, the sacramental-intention machinery of the root README's intention census, run from the inside). Then n. 7, the census locus: "***Thus it must be believed de fide that this man, our Most Holy Lord Innocent X, is the legitimate Pontiff, and the Vicar of Christ in the whole Church*** *— so Salmerón, Valencia, and Albertini, whom Suárez follows… Hurtado… Lugo… **Comitoli, responsorum lib. 1. qu. 99**" — a chain-citation that both confirms the census attributions of Salmerón and Albertini (still missing from the collection) and cites the repo's own 1609 Comitoli and the Suárez/Lugo/Castro Palao entries. The ratio is UPA exactly: "***by this very fact, that the whole Church and all acknowledge him as legitimately elected, his legitimate election is evidently established, so that it cannot prudently be feared [to be otherwise]: therefore it is evident that this singular is contained in the revealed universal proposition, that the legitimate successor of Peter is the Vicar of Christ.***" The acceptance supplies the* ***evidentia*** *that closes the gap the Host cannot close — which is why this man's papacy is de fide while this Host's consecration is not. The rest of q. 24 proves the canonization thesis (n. 7's model applied: "Blessed are the clean of heart" plus the evident singular yields "de fide est credere… quod S. Franciscus v.g. Canonizatus Deum videat"), states the Church-cannot-err-in-morals premise (n. 8, from* Columna & firmamentum veritatis*), extends it to Leo X's Montes Pietatis decree and the approbation of religious orders (nn. 9–10), rules that not even inculpable error can be admitted ("***nefas est asserere totam Ecclesiam, quamuis inculpabiliter errare posse***," n. 13), and answers the objections (n. 12: the definition's certitude rests not on the human diligence but on the divine promise). Q. 25 extends the doctrine to beatification with the proposal-side formula the proposal memo flagged: "***therefore the glory of the Canonized must be believed de fide, because his glory is proposed to the whole Church to be believed*** *[toti Ecclesiæ proponitur credenda]." Verricelli appears by name in Diana's affirmative census (reported in the repo's 1684 Murga entry, n. 79). Classification is* ***1a*** *(de fide by evident containment of the singular under the revealed universal, the acceptance supplying the evidence).*

## Quaestio XXIV — *An sit de fide, Papam non posse errare in Canonizatione Sanctorum?*

**4.** [...] given a revealed universal proposition, **all the singulars evidently contained under it are to be believed *de fide*** [...]

**6.** [...] the presence of the Body of Christ in *this* Host is *de fide* to no one, **not even to the consecrating priest**, for he can doubt of the matter, or of his own ordination [...]

**7.** **Thus it must be believed *de fide* that this man, our Most Holy Lord Innocent X, is the legitimate Pontiff, and the Vicar of Christ in the whole Church** — so Salmerón, Valencia, and Albertini, whom Suárez follows [...] Hurtado [...] Lugo [...] Comitoli, *responsorum lib. 1. qu. 99.* **The reason is: because by this very fact, that the whole Church and all acknowledge him as legitimately elected, his legitimate election is evidently established, so that it cannot prudently be feared [to be otherwise]: therefore it is evident that this singular is contained in the revealed universal proposition, that the legitimate successor of Peter is the Vicar of Christ.** [...]

**8.** *It is proved secondly*: **it is *de fide* that the Pope, proposing laws to the whole Church in matters of morals, cannot err** [...] for the Church cannot err, because she is the *Pillar and ground of the truth* [...]

**13.** [...] **it is nefarious to assert that the whole Church, even inculpably, can err.** [...]

## Quaestio XXV — beatification

**3.** *It is proved first*: **therefore the glory of the Canonized must be believed *de fide*, because his glory is proposed to the whole Church to be believed: but the glory of the Beatified also is proposed to the whole Church to be believed: therefore the glory of each is to be believed with equal certitude of faith.** [...]
