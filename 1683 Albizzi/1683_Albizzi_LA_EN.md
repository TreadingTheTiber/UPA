| ARGVMENTVM. De Schismaticis: Quis dicatur Schismaticus. Quis inobediens Papæ. Quando Papæ Schismatico non liceat obedire. An Schismaticus dici possit, qui recedit ab obedientia Episcopi. An Schisma possit esse sine Hæresi. An Schismaticus recipiendus sit ab Ecclesia, & quomodò recipiatur. De pœnis Schismaticorum. An Constitutiones Apostolicæ comprehendant Orientales. An filij Schismaticorum priuentur bonis, & inhabiles reddantur ad honores, & dignitates remissiuè. | | ARGUMENT. On Schismatics: Who is called a schismatic. Who is disobedient to the Pope. When it is not lawful to obey a schismatic Pope. Whether he can be called a schismatic who withdraws from obedience to his Bishop. Whether there can be schism without heresy. Whether a schismatic is to be received back by the Church, and in what manner he is received. On the penalties of schismatics. Whether the Apostolic Constitutions comprehend the Orientals. Whether the sons of schismatics are deprived of their goods, and rendered incapable of honours and dignities, by reference. |
