## BLOCK A — Cap. X, Argumentum and Summarium 1–12 (printed 45)

### CAPVT X.

ARGVMENTVM. De Schismaticis: Quis dicatur Schismaticus. Quis inobediens Papæ. Quando Papæ Schismatico non liceat obedire. An Schismaticus dici possit, qui recedit ab obedientia Episcopi. An Schisma possit esse sine Hæresi. An Schismaticus recipiendus sit ab Ecclesia, & quomodò recipiatur. De pœnis Schismaticorum. An Constitutiones Apostolicæ comprehendant Orientales. An filij Schismaticorum priuentur bonis, & inhabiles reddantur ad honores, & dignitates remissiuè.

SVMMARIVM.

1. Schismaticus quis dicatur, & vnde.

2. Vnitas Ecclesiæ in quo consistat, & quomodò scindatur.

3. Schismaticus dicitur qui renuit obedire Summo Pontifici vti Papæ, illumque cognoscere in Caput Ecclesiæ.

4. Schismaticus est non solùm qui directa intentione, sed & qui opere, vel effectu se subtrahit ab obedientia Pontificis.

5. Concilium, sine auctoritate Papæ congregantes, vel legitimè dissolutum prosequentes, Schismatici sunt.

6. Schismaticus, in quo differat ab Inobediente.

7. Inobediens Pontifici sibi infesto, & suspecto, quem tamen credit Caput Ecclesiæ, non est Schismaticus.

8. Rebellis Papæ, vti Principi temporali, non est Schismaticus.

9. Negans subiectionem Pontifici, quia probabiliter dubitat de illius canonica electione, an, & quando dici possit Schismaticus.

10. Renuens obedire alteri ex duobus electis Pontificibus, pendente Schisma [1], vsquequò ab Ecclesia definiatur, cui sit obediendum, an, & quando dicatur Schismaticus, & num. sequen.

11. Dubius de valida electione Papæ ab Ecclesia ob eamdem dubietatem adhuc non recepti, an, & quando dicatur Schismaticus, & num. seq.

Obedientia homini tanquam Pontifici, & tanquam de fide tali non debetur, donec à tota Ecclesia sit acceptatus.

12. Pontifex legitimus de fide est credendus, qui talis est electus, & ab vniuersali Ecclesia receptus.

Eique obedientia, sine vitio Schismatis, negari non potest.

## BLOCK B — nn. 1–12 (printed 46–47)

**2** Cumque vnitas Ecclesiæ in eo consistat, vt vniuersi Fideles eius membra, apto quodam ordine inter se cum Pontifice eius Capite connectantur, scilicet, vt in actionibus spiritualibus ad id corpus pertinentibus in fidei doctrina, cultu Religionis, & in spirituali regimine, Caput in reliqua membra quēdam [2] Principatum habeat, atque illa ex eo pendeant, ipsique subdita sint, P. del Bene *loco citato num. 2.* Pueron. *in Bulla Cœnæ loco infra allegando num. 232.*

Meritò ista vnitas scinditur, dùm Fidelis inconstans non vult se gerere tanquam huiusmodi corporis membrum in aliqua, vel aliquibus spiritualibus actionibus ad hoc Ecclesiæ corpus pertinentibus, aut renuit subesse Pontifici, Sanchez *loco cit. nu. 1.* Sanctarell. *loco citato nu. 2.* P. del Bene *num. 3.*

**3** Erit ergò Schismaticus ille, qui renuit obedire Summo Pontifici, vt Papa est, nec vult eum cognoscere vt Caput Ecclesiæ, *cap. Concilia 17. distin.* Io: de Anan. *in* {p. 47} *rubr. de Schismat. num. 7.* Gloss. *in cap. 1. de Schismaticis in 6.* Bonif. de Vitalin. *in Clement. ad nostrum num. 44. de Hæret.* cum alijs per Farinacc. *d. qu. 184.* Sanctarell. & Sanchez *loc. cit.* Caren. *par. 2. de Apostat. & Schismat. tit. 3. §. 10. n. 28.* Bordon. *in Tribun. cap. 7. nu. 7.* P. del Bene *loc. cit. nu. 8.* Pueron. *in Bulla Cœnæ cap. 1. num. 228.* Bard. *in Bulla Cruciatæ par. 1. tract. 2. cap. 4. sect. 2. §. 8. num. 104.*

**5** Hinc deducitur, quod qui absque auctoritate Summi Pontificis Concilium generale congregant, vel dissolutum legitimè prosequuntur, Schismatici sunt, Sanchez *loc. cit. nu. 5.* Sanctarell. *loc. cit. nu. 3. vers. Dico tertio,* P. del Bene *loc. cit. nu. 13.*

**6** Dixi, *qui renuit obedire Pontifici, quatenùs Papa, Schismaticus est:* Nàm si quis non obediret Papæ præcipienti aliquid, vt puta, vt arma deponeret, vel alienum restitueret, is propriè non est Schismaticus, sed inobediens, quia licet ei non obediat, recognoscit tamen illum vt Caput Ecclesiæ, Gaiet. *in Summ. vers. Excommunicatio num. 7.* Suarez *d. disput. 21. sect. 2. nu. 12.* Azor. *to. 1. lib. 8. cap. 20. q. 4.* Vgolin. *in Bulla Cœnæ, vers. Duplex,* Duard. *in Bulla Cœnæ lib. 2. can. 1. quæst. 56. num. 16.*

**7** Pariter non iudicaretur Schismaticus ille, qui non obediret Summo Pontifici sibi infesto, vel suspecto, si tamen aliàs credat illum Caput Ecclesiæ: sic tenent omnes supracitati, quibus addo Duard. *d. quæst. 56. num. 16. in fin.* & Macedo *art. 3.* Caren. *loco cit. num. 30.* Farinacc. *d. qu. 184. num. 11.* P. del Bene *num. 6.*

**8** Nec ille, qui non obediret Papæ, vt Principi temporali, nec exhiberet illi subiectionem in Ciuitate dominij temporalis Pontificij, quamuis iste sit Rebellis, Card. de Lugo *de virtute fidei Diuinæ, disp. 25. sect. 3. num. 36.* Carena *loc. cit. num. 30.* Sanchez *d. cap. 36. nu. 3. & 4.* Farinacc. *dicta qu. 184. num. 11.* P. del Bene *num. 9.* Pueron. *loc. cit. num. 235.*

**9** Nec ille erit Schismaticus, qui negat [col. b] Pontifici subiectionem, quia probabiliter dubitat de eius electione legitima, Gaiet. *in Summ. in vers. Cæterùm quamuis hæc conclusio eruditissimorum Virorum calculo sit approbata, cum grano tamen salis est intelligenda.

**10** Aut enim sumus in Schismate propter electionem duorum Pontificum, & tunc licita est dubitatio, cui nam sit obediendum vsquequo per Ecclesiam non sit diffinitum, cui sit obediendum, & ideò non erit Schismaticus ille, qui nullum eorum recognoscit in Summum Pontificem: Non enim iste renuit subesse Pontifici, sed huic personæ, quam putat, non esse Pontificem, Simanc. *de Cathol. cap. 56. in fin.* Turre-cremata *de Ecclesia lib. 4. c. 14. par. 1.* cum alijs per Sanctarell. *loco citato num. 11.* Bordon. *tit. de Schismat. qu. 5. num. 8.* Pueron. *loc. cit. num. 243.*

**11** Aut sumus in Papa vnico electo, sed statìm secuta eius electione contingit dubitari, an sit canonicè electus, & proptereà ab Ecclesia non recipitur. Et etiam isto casu dicendum est, talem, qui cum alijs dubitat de electione valida Papæ, non esse Schismaticum per ea, quæ tradit Suarez *disput. 10. de Summo Pontif. sect. 5. num. 6.* vbi, quod vsquequo Pontifex non sit acceptatus à tota Ecclesia, non est de fide hunc Hominem esse verè Pontificem, & ei esse obediendum.

## BLOCK D — n. 32 (printed 50)

**32** Quæ multò magis procedunt, quando vnus tantùm se gereret pro Papa, vel Prælato, in aliqua Regione, qui licèt esset Schismaticus, vel nulliter electus, communi tamen reputatione ibi haberetur pro vero Papa, vel Prælato; quia ad validitatē [4] gestorum sufficit communis repu-[col. b]tatio, *l. ## BLOCK E — n. 36 (printed 51)

In hanc autem excommunicationem incurrunt non solùm Schismatici, qui alium Pseudo-pontificem eligunt, cum hi per antonomasiam Schismatici vocentur, sed etiam qui sine alterius Pontificis electione separant se ab obedientia veri Pontificis, Card. de Lugo *dicta disput. 24. sect. 3. num. 37. vers. His breuiter,* P. Thesaur. *loc. cit. vers. Prima excommunicatio.*

Quinimò addit Card. de Lugo, se credere comprehendi etiam illos, qui subdolè malitiam velare conantes, dicunt, se non negare obedientiam Sedi Apostolicæ, sed huic personæ ob peculiares rationes, posteà tamen eius Successori obedientiam præstituros: & proptereà esse addita illa verba: *Qui se à nostra, & cuiusuis Pontificis pro tempore existentis obedientia subtrahunt.*

## Sic-notes

1. Block A, summarium 10 (PDF 57, printed 45): printed *pendente Schisma*; read *pendente Schismate* (ablative absolute). Verified at high magnification; the word ends in a plain *a*, with no tail or macron.
2. Block B, n. 2 (PDF 58, printed 46): printed *quēdam* with a macron over the *e*. Not a misprint but a nasal-suspension abbreviation; expand *quendam* (agreeing with *Principatum*, masculine). Retained as printed.
3. Block B, after n. 12 (PDF 59, printed 47): printed *cap. quoties cordis p. qu. 1.* The *p* stands where a figure is expected, on the pattern of the following *cap. Schisma 28. qu. 1.* Most likely a turned or broken numeral (9?). Transcribed as printed.
4. Block D, n. 32 (PDF 62, printed 50): printed *validitatē* with a macron over the final *e*; expand *validitatem*. Retained as printed.

## Closing note

**Long s.** All long *s* is rendered *s*. Consequently the printer's *verſ.*, *ſect.*, *tract.*, *diſp.*, *poſt* etc. appear here as *vers.*, *sect.*, *tract.*, *disp.*, *post*.

**e-caudata normalized to æ.** The printer uses the e-caudata (ę) very freely in place of *æ*, in roman as well as in italic: *Papę* twice in the Argumentum, *in Bulla Cœnę*, *quęst. 56.*, *de virtute fidei Diuinę*, *de Hęret.*, *quę est inter.impress.* In italic the *ę*/*æ* sorts are often not separable at 600 dpi. To avoid an inconsistency that would read as transcription error, every instance is rendered *æ* (or *œ* in *Cœnæ*). Apart from the long *s*, this is the only normalization in the transcript.

**Italics.** Marked with asterisks for citation strings only, as printed: author names are set in roman and the work-and-locus following them in italic, and that division is reproduced. Two exceptions where the printer italicizes running text rather than a citation are also marked, because the italic is doing quotation work there: the self-quotation in n. 6 (*qui renuit obedire Pontifici, quatenùs Papa, Schismaticus est*) and the quoted words of the Bull at the end of Block E (*Qui se à nostra…subtrahunt*). The whole Summarium in Block A is set in italic as a block; that blanket italic is not reproduced.

**Marginal numbers.** The printed numbers stand in the outer edge of the column, and the compositor often drops them one to four lines below the first line of the paragraph they number (e.g. 3 stands beside the second line of *Erit ergò*, 6 beside *restitueret* on the fifth line of the *Dixi* paragraph, and 21 beside the fourth line of *Si quis verò*). Each is placed here at the head of the paragraph it belongs to, which the Summarium confirms in every case. The single exception is 9, whose paragraph begins in column a of printed 47 while the figure stands in column b; it is placed at the paragraph opening, with the column break marked in its place.

**The opening of n. 1.** The drop-capital block on printed 46 sets *TERTIO datur Inconstantia in fide* on the first line and *propter Schisma.* indented on the second, with *Nàm Schismaticus est ille* beginning flush against the drop-cap block edge on the third. Because new paragraphs elsewhere in this chapter are always indented and *Nàm* is not, the two sentences are given here as a single paragraph; the indent on *propter Schisma.* is read as display centring of a short opening line.

**Accents.** Reproduced as printed: *verò*, *ferè*, *à*, *solùm*, *nihilominùs*, *dùm*, *ideò*, *Nàm*, *statìm*, *licèt*, *aliàs*, *posteà*, *proptereà*, *quomodò*, *legitimè*, *canonicè*, *propriè*, *subdolè*, *latè*, *ità*, *remissiuè*.

**Column and page boundaries re-checked.** Three joins were verified line by line against the images after the first pass, and none drops a line: printed 46 col. b ends *Io: de Anan. in* and printed 47 col. a opens *rubr. de Schismat. num. 7.*; printed 47 col. a ends *Nec ille erit Schismaticus, qui negat* and col. b opens *Pontifici subiectionem*; printed 50 col. a ends *communis repu-* and col. b opens *tatio, l. Barbarius*.

**Uncertain readings.** The author cited repeatedly as *Pueron.* (Block B nn. 2, 3, 8, 10, 12) is clear letter by letter in the roman type but is not an author I can identify, so the reading is flagged once at its first occurrence; it is consistent across all five occurrences. *Azor. to. 1. lib. 8.* in n. 6: the figure is an 8 rather than a 3, though the sort is inked heavily. Everything else in the transcribed blocks was legible at magnification.

**Block B layout.** The body opens partway down column b of printed 46; column a of that page is still Summarium 27–44, so no column break falls inside the block on printed 46. The author cited as *Pueron.* is Giacomo Pueroni, *Commentaria in Bullam Coenae* (1666), cited by Albizzi throughout cap. X.
