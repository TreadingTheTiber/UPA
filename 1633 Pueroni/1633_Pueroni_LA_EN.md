| **36.** Ad rem igitur cominùs accedentes; aperto ore pronunciamus, exigi fidei Diuinæ assensum àb huiusmodi propositione: S. D. N. VRBANVS VIII. verus est vniuersalis Ecclesiæ Pastor, legitimè Diuo Petro succedens : & consimilibus alijs propositionibus, de singulis vniuersaliter in Ecclesia receptis, pro Maximis Pontificibus veris. Cum tamen verissimum sit, | | **36.** Coming therefore to close quarters with the matter, with open mouth we pronounce that **the assent of divine faith is demanded** by a proposition of this kind: Our Most Holy Lord URBAN VIII is the true Pastor of the universal Church, legitimately succeeding St. Peter; and by other like propositions concerning each of those universally received in the Church for true Supreme Pontiffs. Nor is the requisite revelation wanting, since under that universal proposition clearly, formally and immediately revealed — Everyone legitimately elected is a true Pontiff — there are contained, obscurely yet formally revealed, any and all propositions of that kind, and each particular one: this man, and that man, legitimately elected, is a true Pontiff. And the aforesaid things we affirm freely and intrepidly; not so much on account of the gravity of the business, here peculiar — as that the certitude of the Catholic faith depends thereon, since whatsoever Pontiff is universally believed must also be held the infallible rule of things to be believed — as much rather from the very nature of the universal thing revealed. For since it necessarily coalesces out of all and each of the particulars, which equally participate the whole ratio (though not totally) of that universal revealed thing, it must needs be that it includes formally, in that same revelation of itself, the several particulars out of which it is constituted, and which being denied, the collective whole itself would be destroyed. For just as, once it is falsely pronounced that this or that fire gives heat, and so of each, the universal will forthwith be false: Every fire gives heat; so, once it is asserted that it has not been revealed that this or that man, legitimately elected and reputed Pontiff, is such — what remains, save that we say that whoever shall have affirmed this universal to be held as revealed, Everyone legitimately elected is a true Pontiff, is lying? How "everyone," if it be not extended to all, nay to none, since there is no stronger reason for one over the rest? Whereas it is most true that |
| ita esse reuelatum, nec posse contradictorium verificari, quòd illud vniuersale reuelatum non sit. | | it has been so revealed, and that the contradictory cannot be verified, namely that that universal has not been revealed. |
| **37.** Verùm, nè res grauissima videatur hìc labijs gustata primoribus; penitiùs est indagare, quomodò propositio particularis: VRBANVS VIII. verus est Pontifex Maximus: habeatur formaliter reuelata sub illa, de qua non est dubium, vniuersali: Omnis electus legitimè, est Pontifex Maximus verus. an reuelanda dicenda sit tota, secundum prædicatum, subiectum, & copulam; ita vt reuelatio feratur suprà hoc totum: VRBANVS VIII. legitimè electus, est verus Pontifex: vel potiùs solum prædicatum reuelatum intelligatur; scilicet Pontificium verum consequi ad positum subiectum legitimè electæ personæ. Si eligatur primùm, vt eligi videtur à pluribus, iàm particularis propositio de hoc Pontifice non erit reuelata confusè, sed tota dilucidissimè; non minùs, quàm vniuersalis illa de omni legitimè electo; quæ, sicut explicationis nequaquàm est indiga; ita, neq; erit indigens altera; ac proinde legitima electio non erit præmissa euidens, explicans continentiam formalem confusam, & connexionem inter subiectum, & prædicatum; sed erit ipsa pariter immediatè reuelata. Sin magè alterum affirmetur, sua quoq; hoc difficultate non vacat. nàm si sola connexio prædicati cum subiecto posito reuelata dicatur, nil magìs dicitur, quàm verum Pontificium consequi ad legitimam electionem; nihil tamen certi, ac reuelati habetur, quòd hic signatus sit Pontifex verus, cuius nimirum electio legitima non reuelatur. Adde, quòd si solùm credatur verus Pontificatus, huic homini inesse, media electione legitima, quam Pontificatus consequitur, & quæ in hoc homine reperitur; iàm ista connexio fit per syllogisticum discursum, assumendo intermedium terminum, communem extremis; in qua ratiocinatione, iàm patuit, non posse locari diuinæ fidei fundamentũ. | | **37.** But lest a matter most grave should here seem tasted only with the tips of the lips, we must search out more deeply how the particular proposition, URBAN VIII is the true Supreme Pontiff, is held to be formally revealed under that universal concerning which there is no doubt: Everyone legitimately elected is a true Supreme Pontiff. Whether it must be said to be revealed as a whole, according to predicate, subject and copula, so that the revelation is borne upon this whole: URBAN VIII, legitimately elected, is a true Pontiff; or rather that the predicate alone is understood to be revealed, namely that the true pontificate follows upon the posited subject of a legitimately elected person. If the first be chosen, as it seems to be chosen by very many, then the particular proposition concerning this Pontiff will not be revealed confusedly, but wholly and most lucidly, no less than that universal concerning everyone legitimately elected; and as that universal is in no wise in need of explication, so neither will the other be in need of it; and consequently the legitimate election will not be an evident premise explicating the confused formal containment and the connexion between subject and predicate, but it will itself likewise be immediately revealed. But if rather the other be affirmed, this too is not free of its own difficulty. For if the connexion alone of the predicate with the posited subject be said to be revealed, nothing more is said than that the true pontificate follows upon a legitimate election; yet nothing certain and revealed is had, that this designated man is a true Pontiff, whose legitimate election, namely, is not revealed. Add that, if it be believed only that the true pontificate is present in this man by means of a legitimate election which the pontificate follows and which is found in this man, then that connexion is made by a syllogistic discourse, by assuming a middle term common to the extremes; and in such reasoning, as has already been made plain, the foundation of divine faith cannot be located. |

So likewise as to our matter: it having been revealed that everyone legitimately elected to the Apostolic summit is a true Pontiff, it is likewise revealed that any determinate person whatsoever is a true Pontiff who becomes known by the sign of a canonical election, without schism; so that the legitimate election is the judgment whereby to argue and explicate that the predicate of the true pontificate is present in this man, who has the required condition of a legitimate election; and consequently that this singular man, URBAN VIII, true Pontiff, is contained formally in that universal revelation: That whoever shall have been duly elected is a true Pontiff, through the evidently exhibited sign of canonical election. |

| **40.** Id quod à posteriori satis manifestè ostenditur, per imminens absurdum, dato, quòd nullum particulare credendum esset confusè reuelatum in formalitate alicuius vniuersalis, quotiescunq; id per quod ea continentia explicatur, non esset pariter reuelatum.
