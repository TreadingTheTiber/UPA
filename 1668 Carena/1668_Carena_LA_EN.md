Sicut enim Ecclesiam Dei visibilem, esse hanc numero est de fide, ita etiam esse debet de fide, illius caput visibile, cui subest, & obedire tenetur, esse hunc hominem visibilem, pro Summo Pontifice communi Ecclesiæ consensu habitum; & si hoc est de fide, ergo contrarium asserens, est hæreticus, benè *Turrian. videndus in suis selectis disput. part. 1. disp. 35. dub. 5.* vbi quod hoc certius nunc est, quam esset ante tempora Clem. VIII. latè Bonacin. post hæc scripta visus, de censuris in specie *tom. 3. eius operum disp. 1. quæst. 2. punct. 1. n. 9. & Pater Pueronus nouissimè visus ad Bullam Cœnæ cap. 1. num. 142.* | | **73.** **Fourthly, the confessor must observe that one who should say that it is not of faith that this-in-number Pope — for example, Our Most Holy Lord INNOCENT X — is the true Pontiff, is not to be held for a Schismatic, but for a true HERETIC**, as *Suar. videndus de fide disput. 10. sect. 5. per totam* has proved most amply, where he has confirmed this by many Councils, by Constitutions of the Supreme Pontiffs, and by the authorities of the Fathers. For just as it is of faith that this-in-number is the visible Church of God, so also must it be of faith that her visible head — to whom she is subject and is bound to obey — is **this visible man, held for Supreme Pontiff by the common consensus of the Church**; and if this is of faith, then one asserting the contrary is a heretic; well *Turrian. videndus in suis selectis disput. part. 1. disp. 35. dub. 5.*, where [he notes] that **this is now more certain than it was before the times of Clement VIII**; at length Bonacin., seen after these things were written, de censuris in specie *tom. 3. eius operum disp. 1. quæst. 2. punct. 1. n. 9. & Pater Pueronus nouissimè visus ad Bullam Cœnæ cap. 1. num. 142.* |
| **74.** Quintò, circa Schismaticum confessarius eo modo se geret proportionaliter, quo modo *supra tit. 2.* docuimus eum se gerere debere cum hæretico. | | **74.** Fifthly, toward the Schismatic the confessor will conduct himself proportionally in that manner in which *supra tit. 2.* we have taught that he must conduct himself with the heretic. |
| Nam sublata à schismatico censura ab Episcopo, & Inquisitore, abiurato schismate, & hæresis suspicione in foro externo, eadem exiget ab eodem in foro interno: Monereque debet eum de grauitate schismatis, quam confessarius colligere potest. | | For, the censure having been removed from the schismatic by the Bishop and the Inquisitor, and the schism and the suspicion of heresy having been abjured in the external forum, he will exact the same things from the same man in the internal forum: And he must admonish him of the gravity of schism, which the confessor can gather: |
| Tùm ex eo, quod schisma Ecclesiam Dei in Tunica D. N. inconsutili, & in vnica Arca Noè figuratam, & de qua in Canticis 6. dicitur: *Vna est Columba mea, perfecta mea: vna est matri suæ electa genitrici suæ:* vt notatur in Extrauagan. vnam Sanctam, de maior. & obedientia, scindit, & diuidit, vnde Beda in Martirolog. die 25. Nouembris refert Christum B. Petro Alexandrino Episcopo, veste conscissa apparuisse, eique dixisse: *Hoc fecisse Arium, quia Ecclesiam lacerauerat.* | | Both from this, that schism cuts and divides the Church of God — figured in the seamless Tunic of Our Lord, and in the one Ark of Noah, and of which in Canticles 6 it is said: *One is my dove, my perfect one is but one, she is the only one of her mother, the elect of her that bore her:* as is noted in the Extravagant *Vnam Sanctam, de maior. & obedientia* — whence Bede in the Martyrology, on the 25th day of November, relates that Christ appeared to Blessed Peter, Bishop of Alexandria, with His garment rent through, and said to him: *that Arius had done this, because he had torn the Church.* |
