# NOTE — Expansions performed

All expansions below are silent in the text above. Types encountered in these pages:

| Printed | Expanded as | Examples in these pages |
|---|---|---|
| vowel + macron (nasal suspension) | *n* or *m* | `nō`→non, `quā`→quam, `eā`→eam, `unū`→unum, `quidē`→quidem, `euidēter`→euidenter, `contētis`→contentis, `fidē`→fidem, `Tridēto`→Tridento, `agnoscerēt`→agnoscerent, `Verū`→Verum, `Nā`→Nam, `cōsensu`→consensu, `cōmodissimum`→commodissimum, `Veniēs`→Veniens, `tantillulū`→tantillulum, `quenquā`→quenquam, `tamē`→tamen |
| `ī` (i + macron) | *in* | `ut & ī dogmatibus` → *in dogmatibus* (fol. 5r) |
| `oībus` | omnibus | fol. 5r |
| `ꝗ` / `q;` (q + tail) | *-que* | `atꝗ`→atque, `neꝗ`→neque, `Itaꝗ`→Itaque, `nunꝗ`→nunquam, `minimeꝗ`→minimeque, `cuiuscunꝗ`→cuiuscunque, `absꝗ`→absque, `plærisꝗ`→plærisque, `cœlestemꝗ`→cœlestemque, `idemꝗ`→idemque, `quæcunꝗ`→quæcunque |
| `ꝙ` (q + macron/stroke) | quod | `Nam ꝙ huic sessioni` (fol. 4v) |
| `q̄` | quae | fol. 200v (twice): `differentiæ, q̄ est inter…`; `de fide, q̄ quidem…` |
| `q̄a` | quia | fol. 200v: `q̄a p̄ euidentiā…` |
| `p̄` (p with barred descender) | per | fol. 200v `per euidentiam`; fol. 206v `certus esse per` |
| `.n.` | enim | fol. 200v: `Non.n. differt`, `De ratione.n. euidētiæ` |
| `ȝ` / `ꝛ` (r-rotunda + stroke) | *-rum* | `sanctoȝ`→sanctorum (fol. 4v); `earȝ`→earum (fol. 200r) |
| `Oēs` | Omnes | fol. 5r |
| `cōf.` (in a marginal) | conf[essorum] — **left unexpanded**, see sic-note 22 | fol. 211r |

**Expansion policy for `q̄` / `q̄a`.** The printer's own full forms alternate between `quæ` and `quę`
within these very pages, so the contraction supports neither. Abbreviated forms have been expanded
with **æ** (`quæ`), because `quæ` is by a wide margin the printer's commoner full form in the roman
fount used for the body text. Every instance is flagged in the sic-notes.

**Not expanded.** Marginal reference abbreviations (`Deut.4.e`, `Prou.8.`, `Ioan.14`, `Innoc. 3`,
`Resp.1. ad argu.3.respon.3`, `Inno. albritius sum. cōf. li.3. ti.22. q.8.`, `Ange. uerbo. Bap.7`,
`Veniens de pres. non bap.et.c.1`, `Arg.7`, `d.17`) are left exactly as printed, since they are
citation formulae rather than contracted words.

# SIC-NOTES, UNCERTAIN READINGS, IMAGE/OCR DISAGREEMENTS, DAMAGE

## A. Printer's errors and anomalous forms (read from the image, retained in the text)

1. **fol. 4v** — `Romanus Puntifex uicarius esse Christi` — **`Puntifex` sic**, for *Pontifex*.
   Verified at 6× magnification; the *u* is unambiguous, no macron. The OCR layer reads the same.
2. **fol. 4v** — the chapter's opening sentence reads `CIRCA authoritatem uero condentium hoc
   **derretum** aduertendum est…` — **`derretum` sic**, for *decretum*. Verified at 7×; the double
   *r* is unmistakable. (This falls just outside the transcribed excerpt, but is recorded because it
   bears on the printer's accuracy in this forme.) The OCR layer reads the same.
3. **fol. 5r** — `Cardinalis sante crucis` — **`sante` sic**, for *sanctæ* (Marcello Cervini,
   Cardinal of Santa Croce in Gerusalemme). Verified at 6×; there is no abbreviation mark.
   Italianate form.
4. **fol. 5r** — `nempe uniuersalem ecclesiam representans` and `quin & uniuersalem Ecclesiam
   representauerit` are printed **without æ/ę**, while `quod eam repræsentabat` three lines earlier
   is printed **with æ**. All three verified. This is the clearest single instance of the printer's
   mixing; see convention 2.
5. **fol. 5r** — after `Ecclesia Dei uno,` and before `eóque` there is a small raised ink mark
   resembling an apostrophe or an inverted comma. It is almost certainly a stray ink speck or a
   broken sort, not a mark of punctuation; **not** transcribed. Flagged as uncertain.
6. **fol. 144v** — `Veniens de præsbytero non baptizato` — **`præsbytero` with æ**, whereas the same
   decretal is cited at fol. 216r marginal as `de presbytero` **without**. Both verified at 5–6×.
7. **fol. 199v** — `Nónne ante approbationem Ecclesię fides in phophetis` — **`phophetis` sic**, for
   *prophetis* (dropped *r*). Verified at 2.2×; the word is unambiguous. Note that `prophetas` is
   spelled correctly nine lines above on the same page.
8. **fol. 199v** — `quam expertis & demostratis` — **`demostratis` sic**, for *demonstratis*.
   Verified at 8×: there is **no** macron over the *o*, so this is a genuine omission and not a
   contraction I have failed to expand.
9. **fol. 200r** — `Idenque patet de propositionibus` — **`Idenque` sic**, for *Idemque*. Confirmed
   by the OCR layer. The same form recurs at fol. 216r (`Idenque & de quibuscunque alijs`), so it is
   a consistent house form rather than a one-off slip.
10. **fol. 200r** — `Christus secundum corpus` (line 6): the **r of `Christus` is badly under-inked
    /broken**, printing as a faint vertical stub, so the word images as `Ch·istus`. Verified at 8×.
    Transcribed as `Christus`; recorded here as damage to the sort, not a variant reading.
11. **fol. 200v** — `& aliunde quam ex pręmissis, habeat aliquos gradus` — the printed spacing reads
    `hab eat`, with a wide space inside the word. Loose spacing only; transcribed `habeat`.
12. **fol. 200v** — `præmissis` (with æ, line 9), `præmissarum` (with æ, line 18) and `pręmissis`
    (with ę, line 24) all occur **on the same page**. All three verified at 5–6×.
13. **fol. 211r** — `ut communiter tradunt doctores thæologi` — **`thæologi` sic**, for *theologi*.
    Verified at 6×; the æ ligature is unmistakable. The OCR layer reads the same.
14. **fol. 216r** — `Eadenque certitudine credimus` — **`Eadenque` sic**, for *Eademque*. Verified at
    6×; confirmed by the OCR layer. Cf. sic-note 9 (`Idenque`).
15. **fol. 144r** — `ita magno & firmo consensu, ut certa habentur` — printed `habētur` =
    *habentur*, where the syntax would expect *habeantur*. Transcribed as printed.

## B. Marginal rubrics — verifications and problems

16. **fol. 199v, the rubric requested for verification.** Reported to me as
    "*Certa est ex fide conclusio illata ex una credita et altera evidenti*". The **printed text is**,
    set over six short lines:
    > `Certa ē ex` / `fide, cõclu=` / `sio illata` / `ex una cre` / `dita et alte` / `ra euidēti.`

    i.e. expanded: **`Certa est ex fide, conclusio illata ex una credita et altera euidenti.`**
    Verified at 3.5–4×. Two differences from the reported wording: (a) the printer has a **comma
    after `fide`**, and (b) `euidenti` is spelled with *u*, not *v*. Otherwise the reported wording is
    correct.
17. **fol. 200v — `Resp.1. ad argu.3.re-spon.3`.** Printed over three lines as `Reʃp.1. ad` /
    `argu.3.re.` / `ʃpon.3`, i.e. the word *respon.* is broken across lines with a **full stop, not a
    hyphen**, at the break. Verified at 5–10×; the final character is unambiguously a **3**, not a 5
    or a query. The OCR layer independently reads `Resp. 1. ad argu. 3. re. spon. 3`. The sense is
    presumably "*Resp[onsio] 1 ad argu[mentum] 3.æ respon[sionis]*" (Vega's first reply to the
    argument advanced in support of the third *responsio* on fol. 199v), in which case the **final
    `3` is redundant or misplaced**. Transcribed exactly as printed; the reading is certain, the
    intended sense is not.
18. **fol. 5r — the Constance-title rubric is apparently corrupt as set.** Printed over eight short
    lines:
    > `Cur nō est` / `adiectum` / `in titulo, l` / `uerbum i` / `lud Eccle=` / `siam uelē` /
    > `re præsen=` / `tans.`

    Read straight through this yields `Cur non est adiectum in titulo, l uerbum ilud Ecclesiam uelem
    repræsentans`, which does not construe. Two defects: (a) the **stray `l` at the end of line 3**,
    which belongs to no adjacent word (the next line begins `uerbum`), and (b) **`uelē`**, which I
    can make no sense of and which most plausibly represents a botched `uniuersalē` (*uniuersalem*)
    — the body text at this exact point reads `nempe uniuersalem ecclesiam representans`.
    The OCR layer, reading the same marginal, produces `Cur non est adiectum in titulo, uerbum illud
    Ecclesiam si am uelere præsentans` — i.e. it silently drops the stray `l`, joins `i-lud` into
    `illud`, and likewise cannot resolve `uelē`. I have transcribed the marginal **as printed, with
    the line-breaks marked by `|`**, and expanded only `nō`→non and `uelē`→uelem. The intended sense
    is certainly "*Why the phrase 'Ecclesiam uniuersalem repraesentans' was not added in the
    title*". **This is the single least certain reading in the whole transcription.**
19. **fol. 200r — `Argum.4` has no final full stop**, whereas `Argum.2.` and `Argum.3.` on the same
    page do. Verified at 6× on an isolated crop of the margin; the space after the 4 is clean.
20. **fol. 200v — `Euidentia summa alia est a non summa.`** Printed over four lines
    (`Euidentia` / `summa alia` / `est a non` / `summa.`). The OCR layer garbles this, dropping
    `summa alia` from the third line and producing `Euidentia … est a non summa.` The image reading
    is secure at 1.85×.
21. **fol. 211r — `Inno. albritius sum. cōf. li.3. ti.22. q.8.`** The second word is the problem. At
    5× the letters are clearly `a-l-b-r-i` / `t-i-u-s` across the line break, giving **`albritius`**,
    and the OCR layer independently reads `albritius`. I can identify no canonist or summist of that
    name; likely candidates for what was intended (*Albericus*, *Albertus*) do not fit the letters.
    `sum. cōf.` is presumably *Summa Confessorum*, cited by book / title / question. **Transcribed as
    printed; the authority is unidentified.** The companion marginal `Ange. uerbo. Bap.7` is
    certainly Angelus de Clavasio, *Summa Angelica*, s.v. *Baptismus* §7, and is secure.
22. **fol. 216r — `C. Veniens de presbytero non baptizato.`** Secure at 5×; note that the leading `C.`
    is the *capitulum* abbreviation and is distinct from the locus letters A/B/C/D used elsewhere in
    the margins.
23. **fol. 144r — `Gradus.2. fidei humanæ.`** This is the rubric that marks the beginning of the
    **second grade** of human faith requested in Block B. It stands on the **recto** (fol. 144r =
    PDF 351), not on 144v, at the sentence `Secundum locum inter credibilia humana tenent…`. The
    rubric standing on fol. 144v is `Gradus.3. fidei humanæ.`, which marks the **third** grade, at
    `Eodemque modo licet non æquali iure…`; it therefore also fixes the **end** of the second grade
    at `…donec euidentissimis forsitan argumentis contrarium probaretur.` Both rubrics verified at
    2–2.4×.
